AI Review Analysis

TrustCart analyzes large numbers of product reviews using AI to understand what customers are actually saying. Instead of relying only on star ratings, TrustCart generates a simple trust score that combines useful signals such as review quality, customer sentiment, rating patterns and product information. The score gives you a quick way to understand how trustworthy a product may be before you buy it.
